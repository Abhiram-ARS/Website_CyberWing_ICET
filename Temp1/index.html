Knowledge is Power:
Cyber Awareness Hub

Stay informed with essential security tips to protect your digital life at work and at home.

Phishing Scams

Deceptive emails designed to steal your credentials. Always question unsolicited requests for personal information.

  • Verify sender email addresses
  • Never click suspicious links
  • Look for typos and urgent language
Click to know more

Strong Passwords & 2FA

Your password is a key to your digital life. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds a crucial second lock.

  • Use a password manager
  • Enable 2FA everywhere possible
  • Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
Click to know more

Public Wi-Fi Risks

Unsecured public networks are a hunting ground for attackers. Your data can be easily intercepted.

  • Avoid sensitive transactions
  • Use a trusted VPN service
  • Ensure websites use HTTPS
Click to know more

Mobile Security

Your phone contains a wealth of personal data. Protecting it is as important as protecting your computer.

  • Only install apps from official stores
  • Keep your OS and apps updated
  • Be wary of app permissions
Click to know more

Cloud Security

While convenient, cloud services need strong security settings to protect your stored files and data.

  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Manage sharing permissions carefully
  • Understand the service's privacy policy
Click to know more

Social Engineering

Attackers often manipulate human psychology rather than code to gain access to systems and data.

  • Be skeptical of urgent or emotional requests
  • Verify identities through a separate channel
  • Never share sensitive info on a call
Click to know more

Ransomware Protection

This malicious software locks your files until a ransom is paid. Prevention is the best defense.

  • Regularly back up important files offline
  • Be cautious of email attachments
  • Use reputable antivirus software
Click to know more

Secure Remote Work

Working from home introduces new security challenges for both employees and companies.

  • Secure your home Wi-Fi network
  • Use company-provided VPNs
  • Keep work and personal devices separate
Click to know more

IoT Device Security

Smart devices, from cameras to thermostats, can be weak points in your network if not properly secured.

  • Change default manufacturer passwords
  • Keep device firmware updated
  • Isolate IoT devices on a separate network
Click to know more

Recognizing Scams

Scammers constantly invent new ways to trick you, often with promises of rewards or false emergencies.

  • If it seems too good to be true, it is
  • Legitimate companies won't ask for gift cards
  • Slow down and think before you click
